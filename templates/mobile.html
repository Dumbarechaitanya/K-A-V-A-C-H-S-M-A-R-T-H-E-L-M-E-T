15-Second Accident Cancellation Window

When an impact (>3.5g) or fall tilt (>60°) is detected by the MPU6050, the helmet initiates an audible 15-second countdown. If conscious and safe, the rider cancels the alert to avoid false emergency dispatches.

1
Impact / Tilt Sensing: IMU detects abnormal shock or bike fall.
2
Voice Prompt: "Accident detected. Press the cancel button if you are safe."
3
Cancellation Window: Rider presses the helmet button within 15 seconds.
4
Emergency Broadcast: If not cancelled, GPS coordinates are sent to rescue services.
