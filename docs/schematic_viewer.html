🔋 Power Distribution Architecture

• 18650 Battery: 3.7V Nominal (3.0V – 4.2V)
• TP4056 Board: USB-C 5V in, 1A CC/CV with DW01 protection
• MT3608 Boost: Steps up battery to +5.0V Regulated Rail
• Audio Amp: Powered from 5.0V Rail (Max 3.2W)
• Sensors: Powered from ESP32 Onboard 3.3V LDO Rail

📐 Resistor Divider (GPIO 36)

Two 100 kΩ (1%) precision resistors safely scale battery voltage to ESP32 ADC:

V_ADC = V_BAT × (100k / (100k + 100k))
V_ADC = 0.5 × V_BAT
- 4.20V (Full) ➔ 2.10V
- 3.70V (Nominal) ➔ 1.85V
- 3.00V (Cutoff) ➔ 1.50V
